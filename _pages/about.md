<p>
I am a <strong>senior AI research and engineering leader</strong> with 15+ years of experience designing, scaling, and deploying large-scale AI systems across applied research and enterprise production. At IBM Research, I progressed from researcher to senior technical and program leadership roles, leading high-impact initiatives at the intersection of large language models, reasoning, planning, and agentic systems.
</p>

<p>
I am currently based at the <a href="https://www.ibm.com/ibm/research/locations">IBM Thomas J. Watson Research Center, New York</a>, where I serve as <strong>Technical Lead for LLM Reasoning & Agentic Systems</strong>. My work focuses on production-grade, multi-step reasoning and tool-augmented LLM workflows, directly powering the <a href="https://www.ibm.com/granite">Granite</a> model family and next-generation capabilities within <a href="https://www.ibm.com/watsonx">watsonx</a>, delivering measurable gains in reasoning accuracy, task success, and enterprise adoption.
</p>

<p>
Previously, I served as <strong>Program Director for IBM’s Neuro-Symbolic AI program</strong>, leading a global research effort spanning <strong>50+ researchers</strong> across multiple continents. In this role, I defined long-term technical strategy for hybrid neural-symbolic reasoning, reinforcement learning, and decision-making systems, while building and scaling a global research community of over 6,000 members.
</p>

<p>
My research output includes <strong>20+ U.S. patents</strong> and <strong>80+ peer-reviewed publications</strong>, with multiple best paper awards. I am a frequent keynote and invited speaker at international conferences and industry venues, and have delivered 50+ talks worldwide. I also serve as <strong>Jack W. Thompson, M.D. Distinguished Visiting Professor</strong> at the University of Michigan–Flint.
</p>

<p>
Beyond IBM, I serve on advisory and executive boards, including Executive Board Member at <a href="https://centaurinstitute.org/">Centaur AI</a> (USA) and Scientific & Industrial Advisory Board Member at the <a href="https://ncai.pk/">National Center of Artificial Intelligence (NCAI), Pakistan</a>.
</p>

<p>
My mission is to build trustworthy, scalable, and future-ready AI systems that translate rigorous research into real-world impact. I welcome collaboration with researchers, industry leaders, and institutions shaping the next frontier of AI.
</p>
